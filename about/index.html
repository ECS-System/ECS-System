Our Thesis & Philosophy

Why Not All Data Is Worth Extracting

The prevailing dogma in tech has been "ingest everything." The result? Bloated data lakes, runaway cloud bills, and engineers buried under noisy logs. We engineer the antidote: high-integrity triage that captures only actionable signal.

The Economic Reality of Data

In computing, every byte stored incurs compound costs: network ingress fees, tier-one storage, indexing CPU cycles, backup redundancy, and query scan overheads.

When 70% to 80% of generated data consists of identical state pings, uninformative heartbeats, or unindexed noise, businesses are essentially subsidizing digital landfill. We help organisations systematically identify what data creates revenue, defends against audit, or informs operational decisions—and triage away the rest.

The Triage Formula

1. Ingest (Get): Identify true operational triggers.
2. Filter (Sort): Drop zero-delta redundancy.
3. Index (Classify): Structure canonical value.
