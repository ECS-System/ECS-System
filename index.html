The Problem We Solve

The Expensive Fallacy: "Extract Everything Now, Figure It Out Later"

Legacy data teams dump raw streams indiscriminately into data lakes. When 70% of that data delivers zero business or operational insight, you inherit three permanent handicaps:

Ballooning Cloud & Storage Bills

Paying exponentially for BigQuery, Snowflake, and AWS S3 storage filled with duplicate sensor pings, unchanged cache states, and redundant logs. You pay to store it, transform it, and backup what nobody reads.

Wasted Engineering Bandwidth

Your most valuable software and data engineers spend hundreds of hours writing ad-hoc clean-up scripts, debugging noisy pipelines, and waiting on bloated query scans instead of building core product features.

Silent Drift & Audit Fragility

More data does not equal more truth. Without strict classification at the boundary, disparate services use contradictory timestamp formats and conflicting schemas, breaking historical reconciliation when audited.
